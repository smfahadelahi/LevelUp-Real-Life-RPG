# LEVELUP Product Blueprint

## Promise
Turn personal growth into a rewarding role-playing game.

## Core loop
Choose a realistic quest → complete it → claim XP → progress your character → choose another quest.

## Attributes
- Strength: movement and physical capability
- Intelligence: reading, study, learning
- Wellness: recovery and healthy routines
- Discipline: consistency and follow-through
- Creativity: making and creative practice

## Principles
Reward consistency, allow rest, avoid shame-based streaks, keep progress transparent, and prevent duplicate rewards.

## Later features
Room persistence, reminders, achievements, cloud sync, friends, leaderboards, and AI-generated quests.
